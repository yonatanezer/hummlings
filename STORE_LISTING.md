# STORE LISTING

## English
- Name: Hummlings
- Subtitle (iOS, max 30): Make music with tiny singers
- Short description (Google, max 80): Drag tiny singers onto a stage and build a song that always sounds good.
- Keywords (iOS, max 100): music maker,beat,loops,song,choir,creatures,kids music,mix,rhythm,sing
- Category: Music (iOS) / Music & Audio (Google Play)
- Age rating: 4+ / PEGI 3 / Everyone
- Description:
  Drag cute gummy singers onto the stage. Each one adds a looping layer of drums, bass, melody or voice, and everything is in the same key and tempo, so every mix sounds good.
  Tap a singer to mute it. Hold it to change its volume and watch its colour drain as it gets quieter. Record your song and share it as an audio file.
  Three worlds with their own look and sound: a moonlit pond, a candy fair and a sleepy cloud town. 72 singers, 12 secret combinations that start a celebration, and stickers to collect.
  No ads, no tracking, no accounts, no internet needed. The first world is free; one purchase unlocks the other two.
