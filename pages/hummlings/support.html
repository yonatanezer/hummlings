Common questions

How do I restore my purchase? Settings, Restore purchases. A grown-up check appears first.

There is no sound. Check the device volume. Hummlings plays even with the silent switch on. If a call or another app paused the music, tap the big play button.

Where are my songs? Open Songs from the stage screen. Songs are stored on your device only, so deleting the app deletes them.

How do I find the secret combos? Open the combo book (the book button). The dots light up for each matching singer on stage.
