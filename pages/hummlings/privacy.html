Hummlings Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Developer: Yonatan Ezer, ezer.yonatan@gmail.com.

Hummlings is a music toy for the whole family. It collects no personal information. It does send a few anonymous usage counts, described below.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking across apps or websites, no advertising identifier.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, location or photo access.
  • No network requests, except the purchase request to the App Store or Google Play (handled by Apple or Google under their own policies) and the anonymous usage counts below.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which parts of the game are fun and which are confusing, Hummlings sends small anonymous event counts to PostHog (EU servers, eu.posthog.com), for example: a world was opened, a level was finished, a combo was found, how many seconds a session lasted, and that a hint appeared. Each launch uses a random code that is not saved on the device, so one launch cannot be linked to the next or to a person. The events carry no name, no email, no device identifier, no advertising identifier, no location, no IP-based location, no song content and no free text. PostHog processes this data for us under its own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Your songs, found combos, stickers and settings are stored only on your device. You can erase them any time in Settings > Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

Sharing a song

When you share a song, you choose where it goes through the system share sheet. Hummlings does not see it or store it anywhere else.

Children

Hummlings is made for the whole family, including young children. It has no ads, no chat, no accounts and no links to outside content without a grown-up check. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. The anonymous usage counts above cannot be linked to a child or a device, and we do not share or sell them. Buying and the link to this page are behind the grown-up check.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Your rights

Because we collect no personal data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct or delete. Your progress is on your device, and you control it.

Contact

Questions: ezer.yonatan@gmail.com
