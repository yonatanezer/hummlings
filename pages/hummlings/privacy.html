Hummlings Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Developer: Yonatan Ezer, ezer.yonatan@gmail.com.

Hummlings is a music toy for the whole family. It collects nothing.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting that sends data.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, location or photo access.
  • No network requests, except the purchase request to the App Store or Google Play (handled by Apple or Google under their own policies).

What stays on your device

Your songs, found combos, stickers and settings are stored only on your device. You can erase them any time in Settings > Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

Sharing a song

When you share a song, you choose where it goes through the system share sheet. Hummlings does not see it or store it anywhere else.

Children

Hummlings is made for the whole family, including young children. It has no ads, no chat, no accounts and no links to outside content without a grown-up check. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, so there is nothing for us to share, sell or delete. Buying and the link to this page are behind the grown-up check.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Your rights

Because we collect no data, there is no data to access, correct or delete on our side. Everything is on your device, and you control it.

Contact

Questions: ezer.yonatan@gmail.com
