Hummlings Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. Developer: Yonatan Ezer, ezer.yonatan@gmail.com.

Hummlings is a music toy for the whole family. It collects nothing.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting that sends data.
  • No microphone, camera, contacts, location or photo access.
  • No network requests, except the purchase request to the App Store or Google Play (handled by Apple or Google under their own policies).

What stays on your device

Your songs, discovered combos, stickers and settings are stored only on your device. You can erase them any time in Settings, Reset progress, or by deleting the app.

Sharing a song

When you share a song, you choose where it goes through the system share sheet. Hummlings does not see it or store it anywhere else.

Children

The app has no ads, no chat, no links to outside content without a grown-up check, and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
